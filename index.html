Privacy Policy - rain-uploader

Effective date: September 20, 2026

"rain-uploader" is a personal automation tool that generates ambient rain/thunder sound videos and uploads them to a single YouTube channel owned and controlled by the developer. It is not distributed to or used by any other end users.

Data this app accesses

This app uses the YouTube Data API v3 with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope only. This scope allows the app to upload videos, and their title/description/tags/thumbnail, to the authorized YouTube channel. It does not grant read access to channel analytics, subscriber data, comments, or any other account information, and the app does not request any broader Google account data (no contacts, email, files, location, etc.).

Data storage

An OAuth refresh token issued for this scope is stored securely (locally, and/or as an encrypted secret in the automation's private hosting environment) and used solely to authorize scheduled video uploads. No data collected through this token is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
